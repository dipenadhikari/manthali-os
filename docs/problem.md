# Problem Definition

## Problem

Residents and service seekers can struggle to find clear, current, and trustworthy information about public services.

The information may exist online, but it can be difficult to answer basic questions quickly:

- What does this service do?
- Who can use it?
- What documents are needed?
- What should I do before visiting an office?
- Which source is official?
- When was the information last verified?

## Target user

The initial target user is a person in or connected to Manthali who needs to understand a public service before taking action.

The MVP is intentionally focused on information and preparation, not government transaction processing.

## Product hypothesis

If public-service information is organized into structured service pages, explained in plain language, and linked to official sources, users can prepare for services with less uncertainty.

## Non-goals

The MVP will not:

- submit official applications
- make government decisions
- replace government websites or offices
- process payments
- store sensitive identity documents
- present AI output as authoritative

## Success signals

Early product signals will include:

- service searches
- service-page views
- official-source clicks
- checklist creation
- checklist completion
- useful AI-summary interactions
