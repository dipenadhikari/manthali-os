# Seed Data Notes

Seed data should begin with a small, manually reviewed set of public services.

## Rules

1. Use real public-service information only after checking the official source.
2. Record the source URL and verification date.
3. Keep source provenance attached to the service.
4. Do not seed fabricated fees, requirements, eligibility rules, or contact information.
5. Prefer a small high-quality dataset over a large unverified dataset.

## Initial seed target

Start with approximately 5–10 services that are:

- relevant to ordinary residents
- understandable as standalone workflows
- supported by accessible official information

The exact services will be selected and verified during the data-collection phase.
